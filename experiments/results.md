# Retrieval experiment: chunk size × retrieval mode

Golden set: `golden.csv`, answerable questions only. Generated by `make sweep`.

| Chunk tokens | Mode | Recall@1 | Recall@5 | Recall@10 | MRR@10 | Section@5 | config_flag Recall@5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | dense | 0.690 | 0.890 | 0.940 | 0.829 | 0.800 | 0.833 |
| 200 | hybrid | 0.760 | 0.950 | 0.950 | 0.892 | 0.840 | 0.917 |
| 400 | dense | 0.720 | 0.870 | 0.920 | 0.833 | 0.800 | 0.917 |
| 400 | hybrid | 0.780 | 0.930 | 0.980 | 0.906 | 0.860 | 0.917 |
| 800 | dense | 0.720 | 0.880 | 0.920 | 0.833 | 0.760 | 0.917 |
| 800 | hybrid | 0.760 | 0.950 | 0.990 | 0.894 | 0.860 | 0.917 |

## Reading

- Hybrid (dense + BM25 fused with RRF) beats dense-only at every chunk size: about +6 points Recall@5 and +0.06 to +0.07 MRR@10.
  BM25 catches exact tokens such as flag names and environment variables that the embedding model blurs.
- Chunk size matters much less than retrieval mode. 400 tokens with hybrid has the best MRR@10 (0.906) and Section@5,
  and stays under bge-small's 512-token input limit, so it is kept.
- 50 answerable questions means one question is 2 points: differences of 2 points or less are noise.
